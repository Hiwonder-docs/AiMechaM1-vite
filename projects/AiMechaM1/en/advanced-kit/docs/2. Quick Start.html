2.2 Powering On and Charging Instructions ​

Controller:
(1) Controller Charging Instructions: Connect a Type-C data cable to the Type-C charging port on the controller battery box.

(2) Controller Power-On Instructions: Toggle the switch on the controller battery box to the on position.

Mecanum-Wheel Car Chassis:
(1) Mecanum-Wheel Car Chassis Charging: Use the dedicated adapter to charge the chassis via the charging port on the side of the car body.

(2) Mecanum-Wheel Car Power-On: Press the chassis power button located on the side of the car body. The car is powered on when the ultrasonic sensor lights up.

2.4 WonderLens Pro Experience ​

2.4.1 Hardware Wiring and Inserting the MicroSD Card ​

Connect a Type-C cable to the CAM port on the right side of the large model module, and connect the other end to the camera using the L-shaped Type-C adapter.

Insert the microSD card into the TF card slot with the printed side facing up until a "click" sound is heard and the microSD card stays securely in place. This indicates that the microSD card is properly inserted into the TF card slot. To remove the microSD card, simply press it inward and release, then pull the microSD card out easily.

Plug the data cable into the USB-C port of the WonderLens Pro, and connect the other end to a computer port or a power adapter. Wait for the screen of the large model module to light up, which indicates a successful power supply and powers on the WonderLens Pro.

2.4.2 Screen Control ​

The initial interface features six main categories: Stream, Recognition, AI LLM, Media, Settings, and Update.

Tap Settings on the screen to enter the system settings interface.

Connect to a network in the system settings by tapping the local Wi-Fi network and entering the password.

  1. After completing registration, login, and authentication on the XiaoZhi AI Platform, click Agents to enter the following interface.
  1. Click the ∨ button next to the Add Device button, then click Create Agent.
  1. Enter a name for the agent, then click Confirm to complete adding the agent.
  1. Click Configure to set up the functions for the agent.
  1. Click Role to configure the agent role. Select the conversation language at ①, select the character voice at ②, and adjust the speech settings at ③.
  1. Click Model & memory, select DeepSeek V4 (Rich Personality) for the language model option, disable the memory function, and keep the default selections for all other settings.
  1. Click Extensions, check all official service functions, keep the default selections for the rest, and click Save.
  1. Click Devices to enter the device management interface.
  1. Click Link new device, enter the 6-digit code in the pop-up window, and click Link.
  1. Once binding succeeds, a Device added successfully prompt appears on the page. Select Open Source and click Start Using.

The module includes several built-in special functions that can be triggered through voice commands during human-robot interaction. Refer to the following phrases to issue voice commands to the WonderLens Pro and query all currently supported special features: ① What can you do? or ② Introduce your functions.   • Command Examples

  1. News Broadcast Feature: ① Broadcast today's news or ② Tell me about today's trending events.
  1. Outfit Recommendation Feature: What should I wear to go out today?
