Lesson Overview ​

Built around the WonderLab programming platform and the AI Mecha Mecanum-wheel car, this lesson provides a beginner-friendly introduction to smart robot programming from scratch. It covers foundational topics including environment setup, motor control, sound and light interaction, and sensor applications, while expanding into advanced modules such as ultrasonic obstacle avoidance, line following recognition, and robotic arm gripping. Through engaging hands-on projects, the course cultivates computational thinking and builds proficiency in multi-sensor fusion and path planning technologies. The curriculum concludes with a comprehensive autonomous navigation challenge that develops task decomposition, engineering assembly, and debugging skills, making it ideal for STEAM innovation projects and youth robotics competition preparation.
