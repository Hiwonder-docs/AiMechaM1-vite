## 2.4 WonderLens Pro Experience

### 2.4.1 Hardware Wiring and Inserting the MicroSD Card

Connect a Type-C cable to the CAM port on the right side of the large model module, and connect the other end to the camera using the L-shaped Type-C adapter.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_3/section_1/media/image1.png" class="common_img" style="width:400px"/>

Insert the microSD card into the TF card slot with the printed side facing up until a "click" sound is heard and the microSD card stays securely in place. This indicates that the microSD card is properly inserted into the TF card slot. To remove the microSD card, simply press it inward and release, then pull the microSD card out easily.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_3/section_1/image1.png" class="common_img" style="width:400px"/>

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_3/section_1/image2.png" class="common_img" style="width:400px"/>

Plug the data cable into the USB-C port of the WonderLens Pro, and connect the other end to a computer port or a power adapter. Wait for the screen of the large model module to light up, which indicates a successful power supply and powers on the WonderLens Pro.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_3/section_1/media/image4.png" class="common_img" style="width:600px"/>

### 2.4.3 XiaoZhi Platform Account Registration and Authentication

> [!NOTE]
>
> **If the account has not completed GitHub authentication, the module can still be used normally, but available features will be limited. The differences between authenticated and unauthenticated accounts are shown in the table below:**

| Feature | Unauthenticated | Authenticated |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| Model selection | Xiaozhi Lite | Xiaozhi Lite, Qwen 3.6, DeepSeek V4, Doubao Seed 2.0 |
| Bound devices | 10 | 100 |
| Official services | Weather, music, knowledge base | Weather, jokes, music, news, knowledge base |

- **XiaoZhi Platform Account Registration**

1. Click the [<u>XiaoZhi AI Chatbot</u>](https://xiaozhi.me/) hyperlink to directly access the device binding website below, or copy the URL below and open it in any web browser to enter the device binding website.

```py
https://xiaozhi.me
```

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/1.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

2. Click **Console** to enter the XiaoZhi AI agent management platform.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/2.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

3. For a first-time login, register a platform account first.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/3.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

4. After filling in the corresponding information, click **Send code** to obtain the verification code required for account registration.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/4.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

5. Check the box to agree to the terms of service and privacy policy, then click **Login**.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/5.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

6. Select and fill in the required information, then click **Confirm** to complete the registration and login process.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/6.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

- **Logging into the GitHub Platform**

1. On the XiaoZhi AI Platform, click **GitHub Verification** under the <img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_5/image.png" class="inline-icon" /> icon. Wait for the page to redirect to the authentication interface, then click **Link GitHub**.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_5/7.png" class="common_img" style="width:430px" >

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_5/8.png" class="common_img" style="width:400px" >

### 2.4.4 Device Binding

1. After completing registration, login, and authentication on the XiaoZhi AI Platform, click **Agents** to enter the following interface.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_5/16.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

2. Click the **∨** button next to the Add Device button, then click **Create Agent**.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/8.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

3. Enter a name for the agent, then click **Confirm** to complete adding the agent.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/9.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

4. Click **Configure** to set up the functions for the agent.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/10.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

5. Click **Role** to configure the agent role. Select the conversation language at ①, select the character voice at ②, and adjust the speech settings at ③.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/11.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

6. Click **Model & memory**, select **DeepSeek V4 (Rich Personality)** for the language model option, disable the memory function, and keep the default selections for all other settings.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_5/17.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/13.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

7. Click **Extensions**, check all official service functions, keep the default selections for the rest, and click **Save**.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_5/18.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

8. Click **Devices** to enter the device management interface.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/15.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

9. Click **Link new device**, enter the **6-digit code** in the pop-up window, and click **Link**.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/16.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/17.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >

10. Once binding succeeds, a **Device added successfully** prompt appears on the page. Select **Open Source** and click **Start Using**.

<img src="../_static/media/chapter_4/section_4/media/subsection_2/sub_3/18.png" class="common_img" style="width:500px" >
